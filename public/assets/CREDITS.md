## Tiles — `tiles/city_tileset.png`
A 6-frame (32×32) tileset baked from **Kenney "Roguelike Modern City" (2.0, CC0)**
16×16 tiles, upscaled 2× (nearest-neighbor). Frame order matches the engine's `Tile`
enum: Road, Sidewalk, Floor, Wall, Door, Grass.

| Frame | Tile | Source (Roguelike Modern City) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Road | `tile_0714` (asphalt) |
| 1 | Sidewalk | `tile_0022` (pavement) |
| 2 | Floor | `tile_0030` (warm interior) |
| 3 | Wall | `tile_0006` (brick) |
| 4 | Door | `tile_0006` brick + original "open doorway" overlay |
| 5 | Grass | `tile_0888` (grass) |
